## Server rules
1. **Be respectful.** No harassment, hate speech or personal attacks.
2. **No spam, ads or self-promotion** without a moderator's OK.
3. **No cheating, bots or exploits**, and no selling gold, items or accounts.
4. **Keep it safe for work.**
5. **Never share personal info, logins or codes**, yours or anyone else's. Staff will never DM you asking you to sign in.
6. **Post in the channel that fits.** Bugs and ideas go in the forums.
7. **Follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines:** <https://discord.com/terms> and <https://discord.com/guidelines>

Moderators can remove posts or members that break these rules. Questions? Ask a Moderator or Admin.
