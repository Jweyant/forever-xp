## Welcome to Forever XP
Free, community-built tools for **World of Warcraft: Forever**, all at <https://forever-xp.com>.

**On the site**
- **Leveling guides:** fastest Horde and Alliance routes, with time and XP for every step, plus a quest skip list.
- **BIS planner:** import your character and see the best item for every slot, for PvE, PvP or leveling.
- **Talent calculator** for all nine classes, with shareable build links.
- **Quest and item databases**, and every change Blizzard has made to Forever.

**ForeverScout** is our free addon. It records how long quests really take and where Forever's new items drop, so the guides keep getting faster. Get it on CurseForge: <https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/foreverscout>

Everything here is free and open source. Glad you're here.
